# India Startup Diligence Checklist & Knowledge Base

This reference guide outlines the core statutory, legal, financial, and technical documents institutional investors require during due diligence on Indian technology startups.

---

## 1. DPIIT Recognition Certificate
- **Why Investors Ask**: Verifies Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) startup status. Unlocks Section 56(2)(viib) angel tax exemptions on share issuance, fast-tracked patent applications, and preferential access to government tenders under Indian public procurement norms.
- **Typical Documents**: DPIIT Recognition Certificate with unique DIPP/DPIIT number, application submission acknowledgement, and self-certified declaration of eligible turnover.

---

## 2. Fully Diluted Cap Table & ESOP Pool Documentation
- **Why Investors Ask**: Validates exact founder equity ownership, investor share classes (equity, CCPS, CCCPS), voting rights, liquidation preference seniority, and the unallocated ESOP reserve size prior to investment.
- **Typical Documents**: Fully diluted cap table in spreadsheet format (.xlsx), historical board resolutions approving share allotments, current list of shareholders, executed ESOP Scheme policy document, and employee grant letters.

---

## 3. Shareholder Agreements (SHA) & Articles of Association (AoA)
- **Why Investors Ask**: Confirms governance rights, affirmative voting matters, board seat allocations, information covenants, tag-along and drag-along rights, and ensures the company's charter documents are aligned with institutional investor rights.
- **Typical Documents**: Fully signed Shareholder Agreement (SHA), Share Subscription Agreement (SSA), and amended Articles of Association (AoA) stamped and registered with the Registrar of Companies.

---

## 4. Certificate of Incorporation & MCA Filings (Form MGT-7/AOC-4)
- **Why Investors Ask**: Proves valid corporate existence under the Companies Act 2013 and verifies timely statutory compliance with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA).
- **Typical Documents**: Certificate of Incorporation (CIN), Memorandum of Association (MoA), filed Form MGT-7 (Annual Return), filed Form AOC-4 (Financial Statements), and MCA payment challan receipts.

---

## 5. Monthly GST Returns (GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B for Past 12 Months)
- **Why Investors Ask**: Cross-verifies top-line reported revenue against statutory indirect tax return filings with the Indian GST portal to detect undisclosed sales, cancelled invoices, or fake billing.
- **Typical Documents**: GST Registration Certificate (Form GST REG-06), GSTR-1 outward supply summaries, and GSTR-3B summary tax return filings for the preceding 12 operating months.

---

## 6. Audited Financial Statements & CA-Certified MIS
- **Why Investors Ask**: Independent statutory audit assurance on revenue recognition policies, asset depreciation, vendor payables, employee benefits, and historical operating cash burn.
- **Typical Documents**: Statutory Auditor’s Report with Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss Statement, Cash Flow Statement, notes to accounts, and Chartered Accountant-certified Monthly Information System (MIS) pack.

---

## 7. Primary Bank Account Statements (Past 12 Months)
- **Why Investors Ask**: Verifies verified cash-in-bank liquidity balances, operating cash outflow velocity, merchant settlements, and the absence of undisclosed hypothecations or overdraft liabilities.
- **Typical Documents**: Official bank statements in PDF format stamped or digitally generated by scheduled commercial banks for all operational and deposit accounts for the trailing 12 months.

---

## 8. Founder & Employee IP Assignment Agreements
- **Why Investors Ask**: Guarantees that all proprietary software code, machine learning models, database schemas, and intellectual property are irrevocably assigned to the company rather than retained by individual founders or engineers.
- **Typical Documents**: Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement (PIIA), executed employment contracts containing explicit IP assignment clauses, and consultancy IP waiver deeds.

---

## 9. Trademark & Patent Application Filings
- **Why Investors Ask**: Protects company brand identity, software trademarks, and novel inventions from commercial infringement or brand dilution under the Indian Trade Marks Registry.
- **Typical Documents**: Trademark registration certificates or Form TM-A filing applications, examination reports, and patent application filing receipts with complete specifications.

---

## 10. DPDP Act 2023 Compliance & Consent Architecture
- **Why Investors Ask**: Validates that digital user consent, personal data collection, processing records, and grievance redressal adhere to the statutory mandates of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.
- **Typical Documents**: Customer and user privacy policy, bilingual consent notices, Data Protection Officer (DPO) designation record, data retention policy, and data flow architecture diagram.

---

## 11. Customer Master Service Agreements (MSAs) & Signed LOIs
- **Why Investors Ask**: Substantiates commercial pilot terms, Annual Contract Value (ACV), service level agreements (SLAs), termination for convenience terms, and enterprise customer retention commitments.
- **Typical Documents**: Executed Master Service Agreements (MSAs), Statements of Work (SOWs), purchase orders, and formally counter-signed Letters of Intent (LOIs) from prospective enterprise clients.

---

## 12. Cohort Retention & Blended CAC-by-Channel Breakdown
- **Why Investors Ask**: Validates customer payback timing, user churn dynamics, blended customer acquisition cost (CAC) inflation across marketing channels, and net revenue retention across monthly cohorts.
- **Typical Documents**: Historical monthly customer retention cohort matrices, Google/Meta advertising spend reports, organic vs paid lead logs, and CAC computation models.

---

## 13. Granular Unit-Economics & Sensitivity Model
- **Why Investors Ask**: Audits contribution margin per order or user, server and hosting costs per unit of throughput, and break-even dependency on scale and marketing efficiency.
- **Typical Documents**: Detailed financial model (.xlsx) breaking down revenue per user (ARPU), variable cost of goods sold (COGS), cloud computing unit costs, and sensitivity tables.

---

## 14. Product Technical Architecture & System Design Document
- **Why Investors Ask**: Validates system scalability, latency benchmarks, failover capabilities, distributed caching, and third-party infrastructure dependencies for production-scale workloads.
- **Typical Documents**: High-level and low-level architectural diagrams, API interface specifications, database schema diagrams, and disaster recovery / business continuity plans.

---

## 15. Third-Party API Commercial Agreements & Software Licenses
- **Why Investors Ask**: Evaluates third-party API dependencies (e.g. LLM providers, WhatsApp Business API, payment gateways), enterprise tier rate limits, pricing escalation terms, and open-source license compliance.
- **Typical Documents**: Executed commercial vendor agreements, API SLA terms, invoice receipts for platform subscriptions, and Open Source Software (OSS) bill of materials.

---

## 16. Data Sourcing Proof & Training Data Licensing Rights
- **Why Investors Ask**: Confirms that AI training data, web corpora, and scraped datasets were acquired lawfully without violating website terms of service, copyright statutes, or IP rights.
- **Typical Documents**: Data licensing agreements, API data ingestion logs, web scraping compliance legal opinion letters, and dataset provenance audit documentation.

---

## 17. Independent Security Audit & VAPT Certification
- **Why Investors Ask**: Demonstrates enterprise-grade cybersecurity posture and vulnerability remediation by an accredited security auditor prior to enterprise deployment.
- **Typical Documents**: Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) executive summary report issued by a CERT-In empanelled auditing agency and remediation sign-off letter.

---

## 18. Employment Agreements & Statutory Labor Compliance (PF, ESI, Gratuity)
- **Why Investors Ask**: Assures compliance with Indian labor laws, proper withholding and deposit of employee provident fund and insurance, and enforceable non-solicitation covenants.
- **Typical Documents**: Standard employment contract templates, Employee Provident Fund (EPF) electronic challan returns (ECR), Employees' State Insurance (ESI) monthly contribution challans, and Gratuity policy records.

---

## 19. Litigation & Statutory Notice Declaration
- **Why Investors Ask**: Discloses any past, pending, or threatened civil or criminal litigation, consumer forum complaints, commercial arbitrations, or statutory tax inquiry notices.
- **Typical Documents**: Management representation certificate on litigation, advocate legal opinion letters on pending matters, and copies of any court pleadings or regulatory notices.

---

## 20. FEMA & RBI FDI Compliance Records (FC-GPR / FCGPR Filings)
- **Why Investors Ask**: Mandatory for Indian startups with foreign institutional or angel investors to prove statutory compliance with the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) and Reserve Bank of India (RBI) rules.
- **Typical Documents**: Foreign Inward Remittance Certificates (FIRC), Know Your Customer (KYC) reports from authorized dealer banks, and Form FC-GPR filing acknowledgement letters issued via the RBI FIRMS portal.
